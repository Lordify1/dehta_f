Kinda wild that your crew jumped from WhatsApp emojis to a full-blown onsite gathering, but alright, let’s craft something that doesn’t look like it was made during bad network vibes.

Here’s a clean direction for your Armour of Light theme, something that’ll hit different but still stay reverent:

1. Visual vibe
Go for a bold, slightly cinematic look. Nothing too medieval cosplay, just a subtle nod to armor. Think:

A silhouette of a person standing with light bursting behind them.

Or a clean abstract armor outline made of glowing lines.

Avoid anything that looks like a Marvel poster unless you want people expecting Thor to preach.

2. Color palette
Light always wins, so:

Deep navy or charcoal background.

Gold, white, or soft neon accents to represent “light.”

Maybe a gradient glow effect so it feels alive and not like a funeral brochure.

3. Typography

A strong serif or clean modern font for “ARMOUR OF LIGHT.”

Subtitles in a softer sans-serif so the hierarchy is clear.

No script fonts unless you want people squinting and repenting at the same time.

4. Layout
Top: Title big and proud.
Middle: Key visual (light burst, armor outline, whatever you pick).
Bottom:

Date

Venue

Host/Organizer (mention it started from a WhatsApp group but make it classy)

Time

Maybe a tiny QR code for registration or the group link.

5. Extra touches

Add a faint sparkle or particle texture behind the light so it feels “spiritual” but not cheesy.

If you want a Bible verse, use a short one like Romans 13:12 to connect the theme without drowning the design in text.
